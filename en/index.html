What TeamWing does

Players and lineups

Player cards with numbers and positions, lines and lineup variants — drag and drop.

Schedule

Weekly practices, with single sessions rescheduled or cancelled.

Attendance

Mark it in a couple of taps and see who shows up and who skips.

Fees and debts

Services and memberships, monthly charges, full and partial payments, team fund and treasurer.

Games and stats

Friendly game results, goals, assists, penalties and goalies.

Backups

The whole team in one file — save it to iCloud Drive and restore on another phone.

Team data stays with you

No sign-up, no servers of ours, no ads or analytics. The database lives on the coach’s phone, and you save backups yourself, wherever you choose.

Why TeamWing

Made in Almaty by a developer with almost 20 years in financial systems, for an amateur team he knows. The goal is simple: a coach shouldn’t have to keep the team in their head and in five different chats.
