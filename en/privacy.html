Privacy Policy

Updated: November 18, 2026

In short: TeamWing does not collect your data. No sign-up, no servers of ours, no ads or analytics. Everything you enter is stored only on your device.

What data the app stores

Information the coach enters: team name and logo; player names, numbers, positions, phone numbers, dates of birth and joining; schedule and attendance; game results and stats; services, charges, payments and debts; team settings. Optional fields are filled in only if you choose to.

All of it is stored in a file inside the app on your iPhone. The developer has no access to it.

Internet and third parties

The app does not need the internet to work. It has no server of its own, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data. TeamWing does not process bank payments: payment tracking is records the coach makes.

Permissions

  • Photos — only so you can pick an image for the team logo yourself. The rest of your library is not shared with the app.
  • Files — only the backup file you choose to restore.

The camera, microphone and location are not used.

Backups

A backup is created only when you ask and saved wherever you choose in the Share menu — for example, iCloud Drive. A backup is a JSON file without a password or encryption; it contains the team’s phone numbers, dates of birth and payment history. Keep it to yourself and do not share it. If you save a backup to iCloud Drive or another service, that service’s terms apply.

Before restoring from a backup, the app automatically saves the current data on the phone so it can be rolled back.

Player data

TeamWing is a coach’s tool. When entering information about players, the coach is responsible for letting them know what data is kept — and, for minors, their parents or legal guardians. A player who wants their data corrected or deleted should contact their coach. TeamWing creates no public profiles and publishes nothing.

Testing via TestFlight

If you test the beta, Apple may share crash reports and your feedback, including whatever you put in it, with the developer. See TestFlight & Privacy. Please avoid attaching screenshots with real player data unless needed.

Deleting data

Delete the app and all of its data is removed from the phone. Backups you saved yourself stay where you put them — delete them manually.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example, when player accounts and sync are added — the new version will appear on this page with a new date, and the feature will be described before it is turned on.
