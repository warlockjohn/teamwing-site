Support

Answers to common questions. Didn’t find yours? Write to us.

How do I install the beta?
  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the invitation — email or link — on your iPhone, tap Accept, then Install.
  3. Updates arrive through TestFlight.
Where is the team data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and no developer cloud — so make backups regularly.

How do I save a backup to iCloud?
  1. Settings («Настройки») → Backup and restore («Резервная копия и восстановление») → save data.
  2. In the menu choose Save to Files → iCloud Drive → a folder (e.g. TeamWing) → Save.
  3. Open Files and check that the backup is there and uploaded.
How do I move the team to another phone?
  1. On the old phone, save a backup to iCloud Drive or send the file to yourself.
  2. On the new phone, install TeamWing and open Settings → Backup and restore → Choose backup file («Выбрать файл копии»).
  3. Review the backup contents and confirm the replacement.
Can I try the app without my own data?

Yes: on first launch tap Take the tutorial — the app shows a fictional hockey team with step-by-step hints. Your data is not touched. To take it again: Settings → Take the tutorial again.

Can players see their own data?

Not yet — for now it is a coach’s app. A players’ app is planned.
